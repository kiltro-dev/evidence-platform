How it works

  • Paste a factual claim — a statistic, a study result, a jobs or price figure — and pick a domain (or leave it on auto).
  • Atlas structures it, pulls real records from World Bank Open Data and OpenAlex, and runs deterministic checks in code.
  • You get a traceable verdict — every number links to its source, and gaps are disclosed, never papered over.
